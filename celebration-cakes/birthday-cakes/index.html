Questions

Birthday cake FAQs

How much is a birthday cake?

Birthday cakes start from £125. The final price depends on the size and design, and we confirm it, with servings, in your quote.

Do you make children’s birthday cakes?

Yes. Many of our birthday cakes are for children, with hand-modelled sugar characters and hand-painted scenes, from first birthdays up.

Can you add a name or age?

Yes. Names, ages and short messages can be part of the design, piped, painted or on a topper.

Can I send a picture of a cake I like?

Yes, inspiration photos are welcome, though not AI-generated images. We draw every design by hand and make it by hand.

Do you make vegan or gluten-free birthday cakes?

No. We don’t make vegan, gluten-free, eggless or other dietary-restricted cakes.
