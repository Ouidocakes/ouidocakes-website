Celebration cake FAQs

How much does a celebration cake cost?

Celebration cakes start from £125. The final price depends on the size and design, and we confirm it, with servings, in your quote.

Which occasions do you make cakes for?

Birthdays, milestone birthdays, graduations, baby showers, christenings and every other reason to celebrate. Every cake is designed for its occasion.

Can I send inspiration photos?

Yes, inspiration photos are welcome. We don’t work from AI-generated images. Every design is hand-drawn and handmade by us.

Do you make vegan or gluten-free cakes?

No. We don’t make vegan, gluten-free, eggless or other dietary-restricted cakes.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we deliver across London.
