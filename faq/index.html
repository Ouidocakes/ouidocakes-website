Frequently Asked Questions

Prices, tastings, delivery and how we work. Can’t see your question? Ask us on WhatsApp or by email.

Prices & ordering

How much do your cakes cost?

Celebration cakes start from £125 and wedding cakes start from £495. The final price depends on size and design, and we confirm it, with servings, in your quote.

How do I order a cake?

Send us an enquiry with your date, occasion and number of guests, by our enquiry form, email or WhatsApp. We’ll reply with a quote and arrange a tasting.

Do you offer tastings?

Yes. Every cake starts with a tasting: taste the season’s flavours, then we design your cake.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we deliver across London.

Our policies

Do you make vegan, gluten-free or eggless cakes?

No. We don’t make vegan, gluten-free, eggless or other dietary-restricted cakes.

Can I send inspiration photos?

Yes, inspiration photos are welcome. AI-generated images are not.

Can you copy a cake I found online?

We use inspiration as a starting point. Every design is hand-drawn and handmade by us for your occasion.

Are the cakes on your website real?

Yes. All the cakes shown on this website are made by hand, and there are no AI-generated images anywhere.

Our cakes

What makes a Oui Do cake different?

Our cakes are built like a plated dessert: five sponges and four fillings, each a cream of crémeux, mousse, ganache or buttercream with a crunch, caramel or chantilly, finished in ganache or buttercream.

Which flavours do you offer?

Four classic flavours that are on the menu all year, plus four seasonal flavours that change with the season. We share the current flavours with you when you enquire.

Do you make wedding cakes for small weddings?

Yes. Our wedding cakes are designed for intimate weddings and restaurant receptions, as well as grand celebrations.

What are cake drops?

Limited cakes in seasonal flavours, released every two weeks in small batches. Follow @ouidocakes on Instagram to hear about the next one.
