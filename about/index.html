About Oui Do Cakes

Oui Do is a London cake studio founded by two Le Cordon Bleu–trained pastry chefs, Sahar Latheef and Apeksha Kabbe. We make wedding and celebration cakes with layered textures, seasonal flavours and modern design.

Patisserie-led cakes for people who care how a cake tastes as much as how it looks. Two pastry chefs, with kitchens like COYA, Hide, Peggy Porschen, Frog by Adam Handling and Oswald’s behind us: from fine-dining pastry kitchens to your cake table.

Every design is hand-drawn and handmade. All the cakes shown on this website are made by hand, and there are no AI-generated images anywhere.

Sahar & ApekshaCo-founders, Oui Do Cakes

Sahar Latheef

Pastry chef & cake artist · Co-founder

Sahar ran her own bespoke cake brand, When in Doubt, Eat a Cupcake, in Dubai for over ten years: sugar-flower wedding cakes, sculpted showpieces and everything between. She trained at Le Cordon Bleu London and brings the pastry kitchens of Hide, COYA, Avli by Tasha’s and Peggy Porschen to Oui Do.

Apeksha Kabbe

Pastry chef · Co-founder

Apeksha has more than five years in professional pastry, with a strong focus on pastry artistry and recipe development: chocolate work, cake decorating and plated desserts. She trained at Le Cordon Bleu London and WGSHA Manipal, is a mentee for the UK pastry team for the Pastry Coupe du Monde 2026–27, and her cakes lean modern and playful.
