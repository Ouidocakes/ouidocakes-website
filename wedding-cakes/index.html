Questions

Wedding cake FAQs

How much does a wedding cake cost?

Wedding cakes start from £495. The final price depends on the number of tiers, servings and design, and we confirm it in your quote.

Do you offer wedding cake tastings?

Yes. Every cake starts with a tasting: you taste the season’s flavours, then we design your cake.

Do you make cakes for small weddings and restaurant receptions?

Yes. Our wedding cakes are designed for intimate weddings and restaurant receptions, as well as grand celebrations.

Can the wedding cake be the dessert course?

Yes. Each cake is built like a plated dessert, with five sponges and four fillings, so it can be served as your dessert.

Do you make vegan or gluten-free wedding cakes?

No. We don’t make vegan, gluten-free, eggless or other dietary-restricted cakes.
